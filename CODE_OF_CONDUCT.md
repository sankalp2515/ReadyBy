# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss ideas and evidence, give constructive feedback and make room for different backgrounds and experience levels.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, impersonation and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable. Do not post household data, credentials or recordings without permission.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when needed. Raise conduct concerns privately with the repository maintainer through an available private contact channel; keep sensitive details out of public issues. This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests and other project-managed discussion spaces.
